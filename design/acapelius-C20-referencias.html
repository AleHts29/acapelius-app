What feels off in the current screens

Read against your two screenshots. The palette and the no-border rule are not the problem. The weight is.

Sidebar

Four zones, always open

Brand, season selector, two nav levels, theme switch and account take a fixed 208px and can never get out of the way. On a 1280px laptop that is a sixth of the screen for nine links.

Ventas

Twenty numbers before the list

Five function cards, each with a count, a progress bar, a percentage and an amount, sit above the thing you came for. The code itself says only one function is visible at a time, so four of those cards are navigation dressed as data.

Ventas

Two columns with equal weight

The sales list and the "Quién vendió" panel share the same card, type and row height. The eye has no reason to land on the list first, although it is the primary content.

Type

Three voices on one screen

Anton titles, uppercase mono eyebrows (three visible at once) and Inter body all ask for attention. Every reference below has one quiet voice for chrome and saves emphasis for figures.

Six references, and the one thing to take from each

Sketches drawn from memory of each product, not screenshots. Open the links to see the real thing. The point is the pattern, not the skin.

Ventas: drop the second column, keep its three jobs

You're right that the panel reads as a duplicate. It is not quite one, so here is what it does today and where each job goes.

Job 1 → filter

Filter the list by corista

Tapping a name filters the rows. A "Corista" dropdown in the toolbar does the same in one control, and reads as a filter instead of a ranking.

Job 2 → function header

Show unassigned quota, open "Repartir"

The "Sin repartir · 12 entradas" footer is the only way into the quota split from this page. It moves next to the function figures as "Repartir 12 sin asignar".

Job 3 → Equipo

Rank sellers by tickets sold

This is the part that duplicates. Equipo already shows vendió N de M per corista, and Dirección shows who owes. A ranking per function belongs in Equipo's member popup, if anywhere.

Keep

Warm paper, white panels, no borders

Every reference with a warm canvas confirms it. The dark theme tokens already exist and hold up.

Keep

Status as colored text with a dot

"● Debe $27.000" in warn, "● Pagó" in ok. This is quieter than any pill in the references. Don't trade it for chips.

Keep

Anton for the page title and the one big figure

That is the brand. What goes is Anton or uppercase mono anywhere else on the same screen.

Cut

Eyebrows as section titles

"VENTAS · 13 SEPT" and "QUIÉN VENDIÓ" are the mono label system doing a heading's job. Headings go sentence case in Inter 600, labels stay for units and column heads only.

What I need from you before writing code

1

Sidebar direction. A icon rail, B off-canvas, or C top bar. My pick is A. Collapsed state persisted per device, ⌘B to toggle, remembers across sessions.

2

Quién vendió. Confirm the fold: corista filter in the toolbar, "Repartir" link in the function header, ranking dropped from Ventas. Equipo keeps the per-corista totals.

3

Function cards. Replace the five cards with the segmented strip plus one figures line, or keep the cards and only remove the second column. The strip is the bigger visual win.

4

Scope. Sidebar and Ventas only, or the same reduction pass over Inicio and Dirección in the same change. I'd do sidebar and Ventas first, ship, then look at Inicio with fresh eyes.
